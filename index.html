About Us

Everything Scriptured · 1 Corinthians 10:31

ARM Christian Gifts is based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We exist to place anointed, quality Christian resources into the hands of every believer — from Bibles and study books to apparel, bags, gifts and stationeries.

Every item in our shop is chosen prayerfully. Whether you are in Dar es Salaam or receiving a parcel by bus upcountry, our team is here to serve you with care and integrity.

Our Leadership

ARM CEO — Founder & Chief Executive

What We Offer

  • Bibles — KJV, NIV, study and children's editions
  • Christian books, devotionals and journals
  • Faith apparel and hoodies
  • Bags — leather, canvas and crossbody Bible bags
  • Accessories — bracelets, beads, keychains and pens
  • Gifts, wall crosses, anointing oil and mugs
  • Greeting cards and bookmarks
